In respondents' own experiences

Early qualitative themes

2 open-ended contributors
01

Loss can reveal the value of connection

One account of grief described recognizing the importance and fragility of a friendship only after the friend's death.

02

Competence can become tied to self-worth

One respondent connected anxiety to a long-standing need to be capable, reliable, and needed—and to the difficulty of ever stopping the effort to prove it.

03

Perfectionism may connect different miseries

The same account linked relentless standards and limited self-forgiveness to both anxiety and guilt, suggesting that forms of misery can reinforce one another.

These themes summarize a very small number of responses and are offered as observations, not general conclusions.
